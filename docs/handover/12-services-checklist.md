# 12 — Services checklist

_AVMAligners Portal · handover pack v2.0 · 24-11-2026. Replaces v1 of 22-11-2026._

Every service the portal can offer, grouped by the team that uses it. **192 in all.**

This is the other half of the screen book. Each head reads their own group and strikes out what they
do not want built, or adds what is missing. A service left ticked is a service someone has to build,
test and maintain, so striking one out is as valuable as adding one.

## Super Admin and Admin

- [ ] Create, change and disable any user; never delete
- [ ] Give and take away roles, levels and territories
- [ ] Approve a new channel partner account and create its login
- [ ] Reject an onboarding with a reason
- [ ] Make a price list live and announce it
- [ ] Approve a discount above the Sales head limit
- [ ] Release a credit hold, with a reason on the record
- [ ] Change a partner credit limit and payment terms
- [ ] Approve a partner brand profile and its artwork
- [ ] Confirm a recall before clinics are told
- [ ] Approve a scan hold past 180 days
- [ ] Answer a data request and keep the breach register
- [ ] Approve a work instruction and the training that follows
- [ ] Read the full audit log and export it
- [ ] Set working hours, quiet hours and message templates
- [ ] Quarterly review of every user and their access

## Management

- [ ] One read-only view of sales, production, quality and money
- [ ] Cases, revenue and collections by month and by partner
- [ ] Capacity against demand for the next six weeks
- [ ] Quality: reject rate, complaints, open non-conformances
- [ ] On-time delivery and the cost of failure
- [ ] Approve a capital purchase
- [ ] Approve a write-off
- [ ] Monthly management review pack, built by the portal
- [ ] See every open decision waiting on leadership

## Business Development (call centre)

- [ ] Receive leads imported from marketing, with their campaign tag
- [ ] Enter a lead by hand from a call, a referral or an event
- [ ] Merge a repeat enquiry into the existing lead
- [ ] Work a call list with the next action on each lead
- [ ] Record the outcome of every call from a fixed list
- [ ] Set and keep follow-ups; see what is overdue
- [ ] Score and qualify a lead to MQL and hand it to Sales
- [ ] Mark a lead lost with a reason
- [ ] Honour a do-not-contact request at once
- [ ] Listen back to call recordings on their own leads
- [ ] See their own day: calls made, connected, qualified, handed over

## Sales Team — head

- [ ] See every lead, partner and case across both supply types
- [ ] Set and change assignment rules; move a lead to another owner
- [ ] Review an onboarding and pass it to Admin, or send it back
- [ ] Approve a discount within the limit, with an end date
- [ ] Set targets and territories; arrange leave cover
- [ ] Set the lead plan: how many leads are needed, where
- [ ] Ask Marketing for a campaign and record what is expected
- [ ] Give a verdict on the quality of leads from each source
- [ ] Watch partner health: falling volume, silent partners, first case
- [ ] Forecast onboardings and case volume
- [ ] Send the non-disclosure agreement to a white-label prospect
- [ ] Set distributor targets and see them against plan

## Sales Team — member

- [ ] Work their own pipeline by stage
- [ ] Record a visit, a demo and the outcome
- [ ] Send the partner agreement for signature on screen
- [ ] Collect and upload the partner documents
- [ ] Set the default supply type and the price list asked for
- [ ] Submit an onboarding for review
- [ ] Ask for a discount with the reason and the volume promised
- [ ] Help a new partner send a first case
- [ ] See their own targets, pace and incentive as it builds

## Channel Partner (distributor, channel partner, OEM)

- [ ] One login for the partner; no separate staff logins
- [ ] Create a case: patient, dentist, product and prescription
- [ ] Upload photos, x-rays and scans, and see what is missing
- [ ] Record the patient consent the partner collected
- [ ] See each case move: planning, approval, production, dispatch
- [ ] See the approved 3D plan and the IPR chart, and download them to share with the dentist
- [ ] Ask for a change to a plan before approval
- [ ] Track every box with its courier and waybill
- [ ] Confirm receipt of a box and enter the treatment start date
- [ ] Report a lost, broken or ill-fitting tray and ask for a replacement
- [ ] Ask for a refinement or a rescan and upload new scans
- [ ] See invoices and statements, and pay by link
- [ ] See their price list and acknowledge a new one
- [ ] Raise a ticket and follow it
- [ ] Ask for an orthodontic consultation call for their dentist
- [ ] See their own brand profile and packaging stock, where white label
- [ ] Use the status API on their own website, with a one-time code

## Planning Team — planner

- [ ] Work a queue with each case promised by a date
- [ ] Check records and accept, or return the case with exactly what is missing
- [ ] Read the prescription and the partner's saved preferences
- [ ] Build the 3D setup and compare start with finish
- [ ] Set IPR per contact and the set it happens at
- [ ] Place attachments and say when they go on and come off
- [ ] Stage the movement and fix the number of sets per arch
- [ ] Split the sets into boxes and show the run-out dates
- [ ] Send the plan for orthodontic approval with a note
- [ ] Make a new version when changes are asked for
- [ ] Second-review another planner's complex case
- [ ] Raise a suitability concern that pauses planning
- [ ] Plan a refinement or a rescan against the old plan
- [ ] Ask for a scan to be held before it is deleted

## Planning Team — orthodontist

- [ ] A queue of plans waiting for medicolegal approval
- [ ] Open the full setup, IPR, attachments, staging and records
- [ ] Approve a plan; the record carries name, registration number, version and time
- [ ] Reject a plan with a reason and send it back to the planner
- [ ] Set and change the clinical rules every setup must obey
- [ ] Grade cases and allocate them to planners
- [ ] See turnaround, versions per case and refinement rate
- [ ] Answer a consultation request from a partner's dentist, on call
- [ ] Sign off a suitability concern and what was advised
- [ ] Approve a treatment plan for a refinement or rescan

## Purchase

- [ ] Receive a requirement from Inventory
- [ ] Keep a vendor list with ratings for delivery and quality
- [ ] Ask for and record quotations
- [ ] Raise a proforma invoice and send it for approval
- [ ] Raise the purchase order once approved
- [ ] Hand the approved proforma to Accounts for payment
- [ ] Record the goods receipt with lot numbers and expiry
- [ ] Record a short or damaged delivery and a return to vendor
- [ ] Hand received stock to Inventory
- [ ] Avoid a vendor lot that failed quality
- [ ] See lead times and what is overdue

## Inventory

- [ ] Stock on hand by item, lot and location
- [ ] Reorder levels and days of stock left at the current rate
- [ ] Raise the requirement to Purchase when stock is low
- [ ] Receive stock from Purchase into a lot
- [ ] Issue material to a production batch and record what was used
- [ ] Take back unused material and record wastage
- [ ] Put a lot on hold and release it when told
- [ ] Watch expiry and use the oldest lot first
- [ ] Count stock monthly and record the adjustment with a note
- [ ] Hold partner packaging per brand and flag when it is low
- [ ] See what every batch consumed, for traceability

## Production — head

- [ ] Capacity against demand for the coming weeks
- [ ] The schedule board for every machine and shift
- [ ] Raise print orders and mark them urgent
- [ ] Re-plan when a machine goes down
- [ ] Enforce the rule that a patient's later sets print on the same printer
- [ ] Mark a machine down or up; plan service and calibration
- [ ] Approve a purchase requirement and overtime
- [ ] Hold or release a batch or a lot
- [ ] Own non-conformance records: cause, action, proof, closure
- [ ] Start a recall, with Admin confirming
- [ ] Approve work instructions and sign off training
- [ ] Machine effectiveness and material cost per set

## Production — operator

- [ ] See only their own station and its queue
- [ ] Open the current approved work instruction at the station
- [ ] Start a batch and record machine, materials and lot
- [ ] Scan every set at every station
- [ ] Mark the right tray marking for branded, plain or partner brand
- [ ] Record a machine stop and its reason
- [ ] Report a problem to the head without leaving the station
- [ ] See their own output for the shift

## Quality Check

- [ ] A queue of batches waiting for check
- [ ] Check each set against the checklist
- [ ] Pass a set, or reject it with a reason from a fixed list and a photo
- [ ] Send a rejected set back to be reprinted
- [ ] See reject trends by reason, machine, operator and shift
- [ ] Open a non-conformance when a trend crosses the limit
- [ ] Audit one packed box in ten before dispatch
- [ ] Check that a white-label box carries no AVMAligners branding
- [ ] Hold a batch or a lot pending a decision
- [ ] Keep the quality record for every set, for audit

## Dispatch

- [ ] See full boxes ready to pack, most urgent first
- [ ] Scan every tray into a box; the portal refuses a wrong tray
- [ ] Use the packaging the supply type demands
- [ ] Add the retainer to the last box
- [ ] Photograph the packed box and close it
- [ ] Print the label with the patient code and no phone number
- [ ] Hand over to Logistics with a manifest
- [ ] See which boxes are waiting on production, and why
- [ ] Re-pack and re-send a returned or wrong box

## Logistics

- [ ] Book a courier for a closed box and record the waybill
- [ ] Keep courier rates, zones and expected days
- [ ] Track every consignment and show the tracking events
- [ ] Watch deliveries against each patient's run-out date
- [ ] Act on a failed delivery and arrange a re-attempt
- [ ] Record proof of delivery
- [ ] Handle a return and link it to the replacement
- [ ] File a claim for a lost or damaged box
- [ ] See courier performance: on time, failed, lost, cost per box
- [ ] Plan pick-up slots and print the day's manifest

## Accounts

- [ ] Invoice raised automatically when a plan is approved
- [ ] File the e-invoice and fix what the portal rejects
- [ ] Cancel and re-raise a wrong invoice; never edit one
- [ ] Record and match every payment; issue receipts
- [ ] Chase unmatched and short payments
- [ ] Send reminders on a schedule
- [ ] Apply a credit hold by the rule and prepare the facts for release
- [ ] Raise a credit note within limit, with a reason
- [ ] Statements and monthly settlements for each partner
- [ ] Draft price lists and send them to Admin
- [ ] Keep the discount register and watch what expires
- [ ] Pay vendors: proforma, approval, payment, vendor ledger
- [ ] Collections, ageing and the GST working

## Support and Facility

- [ ] One ticket queue for partners and for staff
- [ ] Ticket types: product complaint, delivery, portal, IT, facility
- [ ] Acknowledge within the response target
- [ ] Link a ticket to a case, a box or a batch
- [ ] Open a non-conformance from a product complaint
- [ ] Tell the customer what was found and what was done
- [ ] Ask for a satisfaction score on closure
- [ ] Keep an asset register for computers, machines and the building
- [ ] Log planned maintenance of the facility
- [ ] See tickets by type, age and what breached its target
